👤 Super Administrator
Full system access User management All reports & dashboards Role: SuperAdmin

Operational control Prisoner & staff management Security & alerts Role: PrisonAdministrator
👥 Staff User
Limited read access Prisoner search Alert submission Role: StaffUser

🔁 MVC Controllers
HomeController PrisonerController StaffController PrisonController SecurityController CaseReportController ReportController AccountController
